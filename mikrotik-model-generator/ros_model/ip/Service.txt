# The services are RouterOS's own: they are set, never added or removed (read-only `name`). No
# `id=name` in the header: the entries of open connections (`api` with `remote`) and of services
# RouterOS starts itself (`socks`, `btest`, `discover`) are dynamic and left out.
/ip/service
.id: ro; Id;
name: ro; id
disabled: bool
# RouterOS 7.24 renamed `address` to `available-from` (and still takes `address` on a set); a
# device reports the one of its version, a host as `/32`, and neither where it is empty, i.e.
# open to all (CHR 7.24.5); writing `address` works on both
available-from: o
address: o
dynamic: ro; bool
